# Sections 33–35 Vendor boundary

Canonical implementation and certification traceability is maintained at
[`docs/SECTION33_35_TRACEABILITY.md`](../../../docs/SECTION33_35_TRACEABILITY.md).

Vendor Commerce remains orchestration/relay/projection only. Dispatch owns
MOTORCYCLE and Vendor-owned physical custody, Hauling owns VAN/TRUCK custody,
and Taxi Goods owns CAR custody. Vendor has no custody route or custody schema.
The Vendor-owned RETURN relay uses Dispatch `vendorownedhandover`; no OTP/QR or
provider-transfer authority was created in Vendor.
